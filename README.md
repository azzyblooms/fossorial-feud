# fossorial-feud
whack-a-mole but the moles fight back

Greetings valued soldier! You have been drafted to go fight against the Mole occupation in FOSSORIA. You will be given a hammer and some payment for your work. Smash 'em dead!



MEET THE ENEMIES

Mole
Moles are your basic grunts. They pop up, and they hide. Hit as many as you can.

Groundhog
Longtime allies of the Moles, Groundhogs love to get in the way. If you hit them, they might even try to steal your money. Avoid at all costs!

Armadillos
Kindhearted but misled folks, the Armadillos love to pick up anything they can find---bombs, medicine, wads of cash---anything! Approach carefully.

Snakes
Snakes are evil. They will spit corrosive saliva at your face if left unharmed. Don't let them get away!
